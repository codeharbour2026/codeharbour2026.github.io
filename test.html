Introduction

AI is a machine learning tool that can assist in many different ways from helping with homework, predicting weather, and even translating entire conversations. But how? How does it work so efficiently? How does it get its information? What else can it do? This article will explain all that and much more.

Humble Beginnings

AI was first conceptualized by Alan Turing in his 1950 landmark paper Computing Machinery and Intelligence, where he posed the famous question "Can machines think?" He also invented the Turing test.

The Turing Test is a method to see if a machine can think like a person. A human judge talks using text to a person and a machine in separate rooms. If the judge cannot tell which one is the machine, the machine passes the test. This is considered the beginning of AI as we know it.

How It Works

AI works by first collecting data from various sources in the form of text, audio, video, and more. It then sorts the data into what can be read by an algorithm and what cannot.

Once data is gathered, the next step is for the AI to decide what to do with the data. The AI sorts and deciphers the data using patterns it has been programmed to learn until it recognizes similar patterns in the data that is being filtered into the system.

After gathering and sorting the information, the AI uses complex neural networks to analyze the filtered inputs and generate accurate predictions or responses based on those learned patterns.
